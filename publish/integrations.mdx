Surfaced does not try to be your CMS. Where you already publish somewhere, there are three ways to connect it.

## The API

The [public API](/api/public-api) covers the whole content surface: briefs, articles with their blocks and revisions, the fact-check, and publish or unpublish on the hosted blog.

The usual shape of a CMS integration is to list articles that have passed their checks, pull the blocks, map them to your own content model, and record the published URL back.

```bash
curl "https://www.getsurfaced.ai/api/v1/articles?includeBlocks=true" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_..."
```

Block bodies are omitted by default because they make responses large; ask for them only when you need the text.

## Webhooks

Rather than polling, register a webhook and be told when something happens — an article archived, a long-running job finished. Register them at `/webhooks` through the API.

Webhooks are the right choice for anything that takes minutes: generation runs and audits do not finish inside a request, and polling them wastes your rate limit.

## Direct integrations

Some publishing integrations are enabled per workspace and appear under settings → **Integrations** when they are available to you. If that section is missing, none is enabled for your workspace.

## Reading data out

The same API serves the measurement side — analytics, prompts, sources, competitors, the audit report — which is what you want for a dashboard that puts AI visibility beside your other channels.

For ad-hoc questions there is also an MCP server, so an AI assistant can query your workspace directly with the same key and the same permissions. For a periodic report, the analytics endpoints plus a scheduled job is usually less work than maintaining a copy of the data.

## What not to build

**Do not reimplement the metrics.** Pulling raw rows and computing your own visibility figure produces a number that disagrees with the app for reasons nobody will be able to reconstruct six months later. Take the computed figures from `/analytics/summary`.

**Do not poll a job every second.** Use the webhook, or poll with a sensible interval. Generation takes minutes, not seconds.
