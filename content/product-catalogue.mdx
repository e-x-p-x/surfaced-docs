Where you sell products, Surfaced reads your product feed so that gap analysis and generated content can talk about what you actually sell.

Find it under Content → Knowledge → **Product catalogue**.

Detection is automatic. Nothing on this page needs doing unless something needs attention, which is why a healthy workspace sees very little here.

## The three blocks

**Feed** — the platform, the feed URL, how many products were found, when it last synced, and a freshness badge.

**Products** — what was imported, grouped into expandable categories with brand chips and a search box, 50 rows at a time.

**How it's used** — how many gaps in your most recent analysis relate to your catalogue, broken down by relevance. Each row links through to [Gap trends](/insights/gap-trends) filtered to that set.

## Freshness

| Badge | Last sync |
| --- | --- |
| Fresh | under 20 days |
| Due | 20 to 30 days |
| Stale | over 30 days |
| Never | no successful sync yet |

**Sync now** is available to editors and above, once an hour.

A collapsed **Recent syncs** section lists the last five attempts with their date, what triggered them, the outcome and the product count. Editors and above only.

## What the states mean

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing shown, or "pending" | Surfaced checks on its own. There is nothing to do. |
| Not a storefront | The domain does not sell products. Gap suggestions fall back to your [business profile](/content/business-profile) alone. |
| Unsupported platform | The platform was recognised and cannot be read. There is no retry, because re-probing will not change the answer. |
| Blocked | Something stopped the check. **Retry check** re-runs detection. |
| Detected | Working. The platform, product count and freshness are shown. |
| Couldn't read your catalogue settings | The settings themselves failed to load. Refresh. This is not the same as "no catalogue". |

That last distinction is deliberate. A failed read used to look identical to "you have no catalogue", which told people everything was fine when it was not.

## Words mean one thing each

This page says products, categories, brands and product feed. [Website content](/content/knowledge) says pages. "Catalogue" therefore only ever means products, and never the list of pages on your site.

## If an error message looks vague

Sync failures are shown in plain language rather than the raw connector message. The underlying string is kept in the logs for support. If the plain-language line does not tell you enough to act, that is the moment to quote the feed URL and the time to support rather than guess.
