<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How long until I see results?">
  The first tracking run starts once your workspace has a brand, a website and prompts. A single run gives you a reading; a trend needs several, so give it a few days before concluding anything from a movement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why do the numbers change between two runs of the same prompt?">
  Because the assistants are generative. The same question produces different wording, different ordering and sometimes different recommendations each time. That variance is why prompts are asked repeatedly, and why one run is a reading rather than a measurement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Surfaced tell me how much traffic this sends me?">
  No. Surfaced measures whether you are recommended and cited, not whether anyone clicked. Click data lives in your own analytics, and the two answer different questions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Surfaced see what my customers ask assistants?">
  No, and it could not. Surfaced asks its own questions, the prompts you configure, as an anonymous user. Nothing about real users' conversations is visible to it or to us.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is my own domain in the Sources table?">
  Because it is a cited source like any other, and seeing which of your pages get quoted is half the value of the screen. It is highlighted rather than removed, and it counts toward the totals.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between a gap and an action?">
  A gap is a finding: something the answers get wrong or leave out. An action is work: something a person is going to do about it, with an owner and a state. Accepting a suggestion on a gap is how one becomes the other.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does marking a gap handled fix it?">
  No. It records what you decided, not what the assistants now say. Whether the answers changed is what the **Measuring** phase and the next tracking run tell you, and expect a lag of at least one run, usually more, because the engines have to re-read your site first. [More](/insights/gap-trends).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why can't I see Share of Voice?">
  A dash means nothing was measured, not that you scored zero. If no brand at all was detected in the period, there is no denominator. A real zero, where competitors were named and you were not, is shown as 0% with that wording.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many prompts should I track?">
  Enough to cover the questions that decide a purchase, which for most businesses is a few dozen rather than hundreds. Prompts you add for completeness dilute your visibility figure without telling you anything. Start with the questions your sales team gets.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I track branded prompts?">
  A few, to see how you are described when someone already knows you. Keep them separate in your reading: branded prompts return your brand nearly always, so blending them makes your visibility look far healthier than it is. [More](/metrics/periods-and-filters).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I track more than one brand?">
  Yes, one workspace per brand. Workspaces have their own prompts, competitors, members and settings, and sit under one organization for billing. [More](/account/members-and-roles).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which AI platforms does Surfaced track?">
  Twelve: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Grok, Mistral, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Bing Copilot, Google Search and Google News. Prompts are tracked every day on every plan. The Free plan tracks ChatGPT only; paid plans can track all twelve. You choose which ones under [Tracking defaults](/set-up/tracking-defaults); plans are on the [pricing page](https://www.getsurfaced.ai/pricing).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I go over my plan's limits?">
  If a night's run would go over your monthly tracked-response allowance, that run does not happen at all and a banner says tracking has stopped until the allowance resets at the start of the month or you upgrade. Nothing is deleted. History older than your plan's window stops being reachable rather than being erased, and raising the plan brings it back. [More](/account/billing-and-usage).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export the data?">
  Analytics exports as a PDF carrying your selected period, and gap rows export to CSV from the bulk bar. There is also a [public API](/api/public-api) for programmatic access.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does the audit only check a few pages?">
  It reads your homepage and your priority pages rather than crawling everything, which keeps a re-check to minutes and keeps the score focused on the pages that get cited. It is a sample, and the header tells you how many pages it covered.
</Accordion>
