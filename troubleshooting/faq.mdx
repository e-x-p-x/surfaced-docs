title: "FAQ"
description: "Short answers to the questions asked most often in the first month."
---

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How long until I see results?">
  The first tracking run starts once your workspace has a brand, a website and prompts. A single run gives you a reading; a trend needs several, so give it a few days before concluding anything from a movement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why do the numbers change between two runs of the same prompt?">
  Because the assistants are generative. The same question produces different wording, different ordering and sometimes different recommendations each time. That variance is why prompts are asked repeatedly and why a single run is a reading rather than a measurement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Surfaced tell me how much traffic this sends me?">
  No. Surfaced measures whether you are recommended and cited, not whether anyone clicked. Click data lives in your own analytics; the two answer different questions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Surfaced see what my customers ask assistants?">
  No, and it could not. Surfaced asks its own questions — the prompts you configure — as an anonymous user. Nothing about real users' conversations is visible to it or to us.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is my own domain in the Sources table?">
  Because it is a cited source like any other, and seeing which of your pages get quoted is half the value of the screen. It is highlighted rather than removed, and it counts toward the totals.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between a gap and an action?">
  A gap is a finding: something the answers get wrong or leave out. An action is work: something a person is going to do about it, with an owner and a state. Accepting a suggestion on a gap is how one becomes the other.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does marking a gap 'actioned' fix it?">
  No. It records that you did something. Whether the answers changed is what the next tracking run tells you — expect a lag of at least a run, usually more, because the engines have to re-read your site first.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why can't I see Share of Voice?">
  A dash means nothing was measured, not that you scored zero. If no brand at all was detected in the period, there is no denominator. A real zero — competitors named, you not — is shown as 0% with that wording.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many prompts should I track?">
  Enough to cover the questions that decide a purchase, which for most businesses is a few dozen rather than hundreds. Prompts you add for completeness dilute your visibility figure without telling you anything. Start with the questions your sales team actually gets.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I track branded prompts?">
  A few, to see how you are described when someone already knows you. But keep them separate in your reading: branded prompts return your brand nearly always, so blending them makes your visibility look far healthier than it is. [More](/metrics/periods-and-filters).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I track more than one brand?">
  Yes — one workspace per brand. Workspaces have their own prompts, competitors, members and settings, and sit under one organization for billing. [More](/account/members-and-roles).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I go over my plan's limits?">
  Prompts above the cap are paused rather than deleted, with a banner saying how many, and history older than your plan's window stops being reachable rather than being erased. Raising the plan brings both back. [More](/account/billing-and-usage).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export the data?">
  Analytics exports as a PDF carrying your selected period, and gap rows export to CSV from the bulk bar. There is also a [public API](/api/public-api) for programmatic access.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does the audit only check a few pages?">
  It reads your homepage and your priority pages rather than crawling everything, which keeps a re-check to minutes and keeps the score focused on the pages that actually get cited. It is a sample, and the header tells you how many pages it covered.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
