The sidebar has three sections: Insights, Improve and Content, with Home at the top and Workspace settings in the sidebar footer. This page lists them in the order you see them, so you can go from a screen you are looking at to the page that explains it.

## Insights: what is happening

Measurement. Nothing here changes anything; it tells you where you stand.

| Screen | What it answers | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analytics | How are we doing overall? | [Analytics](/insights/analytics) |
| Prompts | Which questions do we win and lose? | [Prompts](/insights/prompts) |
| Sources | What are the models reading? | [Sources](/insights/sources) |
| Competitors | Who else is being named? | [Competitors](/insights/competitors) |
| Gap trends | What do the answers get wrong about us? | [Gap trends](/insights/gap-trends) |
| Audit report | Can assistants read and quote our site? | [Audit report](/insights/audit-report) |

## Improve: what to do about it

| Screen | What it answers | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actions | What is on my list? | [Actions](/improve/actions) |

Actions is a single queue. Findings from across Insights arrive here as work, each with an owner and a status, so there is one list rather than a to-do list on every Insights screen. Three cards at the top group the work: Content gaps (from Gap trends), Website audit (from the Audit report) and Backlink opportunities (from Sources).

## Workspace settings: how it is configured

Workspace settings sit in the sidebar footer, not in the main sections. They hold the brand, the tracking set-up, the knowledge the content generator draws on and the content-engine configuration. See [Set up](/set-up/workspace).

| Group | Screens | Page |

| Setup | General, Tracking defaults, Blog | [Workspace](/set-up/workspace), [Tracking defaults](/set-up/tracking-defaults), [Hosted blog](/publish/hosted-blog) |

## Account and organization

Your own account and the organization sit behind your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar. Under **You**: Setup Guide, Account settings, API keys and Connect AI assistant (see [MCP server](/api/mcp)). Under **Organization**: General, Members ([Members and roles](/account/members-and-roles)), Billing & Usage ([Billing and usage](/account/billing-and-usage)) and Referrals ([Referrals](/account/referrals)).

## Finding the right screen from a question

- *Are we being recommended?* → [Analytics](/insights/analytics), brand visibility.
- *Why did our number drop?* → [Prompts](/insights/prompts), grouped by brand visibility, then read the answers.
- *Who is beating us, and where?* → [Competitors](/insights/competitors), then click the rival.
- *Why are we never cited?* → [Audit report](/insights/audit-report).
- *What should I do first?* → [Actions](/improve/actions).
