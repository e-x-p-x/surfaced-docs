Setup is four screens and a wait. The decisions that matter come after, when you choose which questions are worth tracking every night.

<Steps>

<Step title="Name your company and website">
  Sign-up pre-fills both from your email domain where it can. The website is not cosmetic — the audit reads it, and a workspace without a valid one shows onboarding instead of Insights on every screen.

  You can skip the website, but you will have no tracking until you add one.
</Step>

<Step title="Check what Surfaced read about you">
  Your site is analysed into an industry, a product type, an audience, likely competitors and keywords. Every field is editable, and it is worth two minutes: everything suggested later is built on it.

  If the analysis fails or is wide of the mark, enter the details by hand.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose your prompts">
  Surfaced generates a set of questions and tells you how many you can run inside your plan's monthly response allowance.

  Pick the ones a real buyer would type before choosing a supplier. Leave the rest — you can add more later, and [Prompts](/set-up/prompts) explains what makes a good one.
</Step>

<Step title="Run the first tracking run">
  This usually takes a few minutes. The first run deliberately asks every supported platform, so you get a complete picture before settling into your workspace defaults.

  Results appear as they land; you do not have to wait on the page.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Then look at four things, in this order

**[Analytics](/insights/analytics).** Your five headline figures. Read [brand visibility](/metrics/brand-visibility) first, and the note on [periods and filters](/metrics/periods-and-filters) before you draw any conclusion from it — the default view is narrower than most people assume.

**[Prompts](/insights/prompts), grouped by brand visibility.** This turns one number into a list of questions you win and questions you lose. Open two or three of the losses and read the actual answers.

**[Competitors](/insights/competitors).** Click the rival that appears most. You get every prompt where they are recommended and you are not — the most directly useful list in the product.

**[Audit report](/insights/audit-report).** The one screen you can act on today without waiting for another run. It tells you whether assistants can read and quote your site at all.

## What to expect in week one

**A single run is a reading, not a measurement.** The same question asked twice returns different wording and sometimes different recommendations. Give it several days before treating a movement as a trend.

**Platforms differ more than days do.** Before concluding something changed, check the per-platform charts — the spread between two assistants on the same day is usually wider.

**Nothing you publish will show up yet.** The engines have to re-read your site before a new page can be cited, and then a run has to ask the questions again. Judge content work over several runs.
