Audit report checks your website rather than the answers. Of everything in Surfaced it is the most directly actionable: each finding names a page, the value found on it, and what to change.

## The score

One [audit score](/metrics/audit-score) out of 100 with a letter grade, and three sub-scores:

- **Foundation.** Site-wide setup: HTTPS, whether AI crawlers are allowed in, your sitemap, your schema. Fixed once, not per page.
- **On-page.** Whether each page is labelled so an assistant knows what it is about: title tags, meta descriptions, headings, declared language.
- **Content.** Whether the content is good enough to be worth citing: depth, authority, sourcing.

A sub-score is coloured by whether it contains a critical failure, not by how high it is. An amber-looking 68 shown in red has something critical in it.

## The tabs

**Foundation**, **On-page** and **Content** each hold a table of findings, one row per page per issue, criticals first:

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Page / URL | Which page the finding is on. |
| Severity | **Critical** or **Needs work**. Passing checks are listed separately. |
| Issue | What is wrong. |
| Fix | What to change. |

Expanding a row shows the problem and the fix side by side, with the value found on your page.

**Off-page** is a manual best-practice checklist rather than an automated scan. Its ticks are stored in your browser, so they are per-person and per-device.

## Actions

**Re-check now** runs the audit again, with a few minutes' cooldown between runs. It is two chained jobs, so it takes minutes rather than seconds, and the page says so if it runs long.

**Create brief** on a content finding turns it into a writing brief with page-specific instructions, and sends you to [Briefs](/content/briefs). It never edits or publishes the page itself.

There is also a **generate llms.txt** helper, and a sitemap prompt when none is found. Saving a sitemap triggers a re-check.

## What it does and does not cover

**It reads a sample.** Your homepage and priority pages, not the whole site. A good score on five pages says nothing about five hundred.

**It is not live.** The audit reruns on an explicit re-check or on the nightly scrape. The header says when the current one was taken.

**"Link authority" means internal linking.** The audit reads your own pages and the links between them. No backlink data is involved, so this is not a domain-authority score.

**Competitive findings are not here.** What rivals are credited with lives on [Gap trends](/insights/gap-trends), deliberately, so the two screens do not disagree about the same thing.

**A page that passes everything and is never cited is flagged** as a warning rather than a failure. Passing every technical check while no assistant quotes you is the specific situation this product exists for.

## Before the first audit

"Run your first audit" with a score of `—` means no audit has completed yet. Run one; it needs nothing but a reachable website.
