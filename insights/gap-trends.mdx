A gap is a tracked question where the AI answers leave you out or get you wrong. Gap trends shows every open gap, how often the answers still miss you, and whether that is improving after the work you did.

The page updates itself after each tracking run; the header says when the next update is due. **Check answers** re-runs the analysis on demand.

## What is on the page

From top to bottom:

- **Filters.** Period (for example 12 weeks), AI engine, topic and market, plus **Share**.
- **Summary cards.** _Answers that miss you_ (the share of answers to your gap questions that leave you out), _Open_, _Improving_, _Closed in period_ and _Reopened_, each with its change over the period.
- **Answers that miss you, 28-day rolling.** A trend chart you can switch between _Miss rate_ and _Active gaps_. Numbered markers show your own events, such as work started on a gap and articles published, so you can see whether the line moved after them.
- **Movers.** _Improving the most_, _Getting worse_, _New gaps_ and _Closed_, each with the miss rate before and after.
- **Per AI engine.** How often each engine misses you over the last 28 days, and the change.
- **Named instead of you.** The brands the answers name for your gap questions, their share, and the engine where each is strongest.
- **Where you're missing.** A topic × AI engine grid of miss rates.
- **All gaps.** One row per gap with topic, market, miss rate, change, a weekly sparkline, state (open or closed) and the status of any action on it.
- **Findings.** The detailed list behind the gaps, with Filters and **Export CSV**. Each finding says what is wrong, which question it comes from, how long it has been missing, the share of answers it is absent from, and its priority. **Weekly activity** and **Gap history** show what opened, improved, closed and reopened week by week.

## Reading a finding

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Kind | What is wrong, for example **Not mentioned** (the answers leave you out) or **Misconception** (they get something about you wrong). |
| Catalogue fit | Whether the gap is about something you sell: **In catalogue**, **Adjacent** or **Out of catalogue**. Read from your [Product catalogue](/content/product-catalogue) and [Business profile](/content/business-profile). |
| Missing since | How long the answers have left you out. |
| Absent | The share of recent answers (last 28 days) that miss you, and how many answers that is based on. |
| Priority | How much fixing it is worth: critical, high, medium or low. |
| Action | **View action** opens the work item for this gap in [Actions](/improve/actions). |

## A gap closes in the answers, not in the to-do list

Starting work, publishing an article or marking an action done does not close a gap. A gap moves to improving or closed only when later tracking runs show the answers changing, and that takes at least one more run, usually several, because the engines have to re-read your site first. The markers on the trend chart are there so you can check whether that happened.

## Locked gaps

A gap found only on platforms your workspace does not track is a **locked finding**: shown as a teaser, and excluded from the counts and from every action on the page.

This is the most confusing thing on the screen. A workspace can be sitting on outstanding critical gaps while the counts read zero, because none of them were found on a platform you track. The page says when locked findings exist.

Nothing is locked if you track no platforms at all. Locking only begins once at least one is tracked.

## When an analysis fails

A failed analysis does not wipe your gaps. The page keeps showing the last analysis that completed and puts an amber notice above it, so a bad night never looks like a clean slate.

## From gap to work

Gaps become work in [Actions](/improve/actions), under the **Content gaps** card. From there a gap can become a brief in [Briefs](/content/briefs). If [Content Agent](/content/content-agent) is on (its switch and **Settings** sit above the findings list), it prepares that work for you to approve.

## What "no gaps detected" means

It means this analysis found none in the answers it read. It is not a verification that every claim about you is true, and the page says so. Read it as "nothing stood out" rather than "we checked everything".
