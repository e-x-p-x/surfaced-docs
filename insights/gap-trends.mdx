---
title: "Gap trends"
description: "What the AI answers get wrong or leave out about you, how severe each gap is, and how that is changing."
---

A gap is something the answers say about you that is wrong, missing or out of date, or something a competitor is credited with that you also do. Gap trends shows the current set and how it has moved across runs.

## The six kinds of gap

| Type | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Factual inaccuracy | The answer states something about you that is not true. |
| Missing information | Something a buyer needs is absent. |
| Outdated information | True once, not now. |
| Competitor advantage | A rival is credited with something you also do. |
| Coverage gap | A whole topic where you are never mentioned. |
| Sentiment issue | A recurring negative framing. |

## Severity

**Critical → High → Medium → Low.**

## Phase: where a finding is in the work

The tabs across the top are phases, and they are the main way the table is sliced. **All findings** shows everything; the rest count what is in each phase.

| Phase | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **To decide** | Nobody has decided what to do about it yet. |
| **Being addressed** | Work is under way. |
| **Measuring** | The work is done and Surfaced is waiting to see whether the answers moved. |
| **Verified** | The answers moved. |

Measuring is the phase people are surprised by. Finishing the work does not finish the finding, because the point was never the work: it was whether the assistants changed what they say. That takes at least one more tracking run, and usually more.

## Disposition: what you decided

Separately from the phase, each finding carries what you decided about it.

| Disposition | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Untouched. |
| **Reviewed** | Someone has looked. |
| **Handled outside Surfaced** | You dealt with it somewhere else and want it off the list. |
| **Dismissed** | Explicitly decided against. |

<Note>
  Accepting a brief against a gap does not close it, and neither does marking it handled. Those record what you decided, not what the assistants now say. Whether anything changed is what the **Measuring** phase is for, and what the next tracking run tells you.
</Note>

## Findings that need attention

A finding can also be flagged for a reason that is not a phase: an approval is required, work is blocked, work failed and needs review, its evidence has gone stale, a measurement is overdue, or there is active work on something already handled or dismissed.

These are the rows worth opening first. A blocked or failed item will sit in **Being addressed** forever otherwise, looking like progress.

## Locked gaps

A gap found only on platforms your workspace does not track is a **locked finding**: shown as a teaser, and excluded from the counts and from every action on the page.

This is the most confusing thing on the screen. A workspace can be sitting on outstanding critical gaps while the counts read zero, because none of them were found on a platform you track. The page says when locked findings exist.

Nothing is locked if you track no platforms at all. Locking only begins once at least one is tracked.

## Running an analysis

**Run analysis** becomes available once you have a completed tracking run, since there is nothing to analyse before that. It can be cancelled, reports progress, and says so if it appears stuck.

A failed run does not wipe your gaps. The page keeps showing the last analysis that completed and puts an amber notice above it, so a bad night never looks like a clean slate.

## Actions

Per row: mark it reviewed, mark it handled outside Surfaced, dismiss it, or accept a suggestion, which creates a brief in [Content](/content/briefs). Findings can also be selected in bulk, updated together and exported.

Locked findings offer none of these.

## What "no gaps detected" means

It means this analysis found none in the answers it read. It is not a verification that every claim about you is true, and the page says so. Read it as "nothing stood out" rather than "we checked everything".
