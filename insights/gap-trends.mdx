title: "Gap trends"
description: "What the AI answers get wrong or leave out about you, how severe each gap is, and how that is changing."
---

A gap is something the answers say about you that is wrong, missing or out of date — or something a competitor is credited with that you are not. Gap trends shows the current set and how it has moved across runs.

## The six kinds of gap

| Type | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Factual inaccuracy | The answer states something about you that is not true. |
| Missing information | Something a buyer needs is simply absent. |
| Outdated information | True once, not now. |
| Competitor advantage | A rival is credited with something you also do. |
| Coverage gap | A whole topic where you are never mentioned. |
| Sentiment issue | A recurring negative framing. |

## Severity and status

Severity runs **critical → high → medium → low**. Status runs:

- **New** — untouched. Outstanding.
- **Reviewed** — someone has looked. Still outstanding.
- **Actioned** — you recorded that you addressed it. Terminal.
- **Dismissed** — explicitly decided against. Terminal, and hidden from the default view.

The filter also offers **Addressed**, which is actioned and dismissed together — the two terminal states — so that clicking a card reading "5 of 10 addressed" shows you all five.

<Note>
  Accepting a brief against a gap does not close it. "Actioned" is a bookkeeping flag you set, not a verification that the answers changed. Whether they changed is what the next tracking run tells you.
</Note>

## The cards

Total gaps, critical, high priority, and progress. All four are clickable filters: clicking one shows exactly the rows it counted.

**Progress** counts both terminal states, so dismissing everything reaches 100%. That is the intended behaviour — a dismissed gap is a decision, not a debt — but it does mean progress measures triage, not improvement.

## Locked gaps

A gap found only on platforms your workspace does not track is **locked**: shown as a teaser with a padlock, and excluded from every card, every count and every action on the page.

This is the single most confusing thing on the screen, and it is worth stating plainly: a workspace can be sitting on dozens of outstanding critical gaps while the page reads Critical 0, because none of them were found on a tracked platform. The total card shows "+N locked" when that is the case.

Two related details:

- Nothing is locked if you track no platforms at all. Locking only begins once at least one is tracked.
- The "All severity" and "All status" dropdown options count locked gaps; the individual options do not. "All" will not equal the sum of its siblings on a workspace with locked rows.

## Running an analysis

**Run analysis** is available once you have a completed tracking run — there is nothing to analyse before that. It can be cancelled, reports progress, and says so if it appears stuck.

A failed run does not wipe your gaps. The page keeps showing the last analysis that completed and puts an amber notice above it, so a bad night never looks like a clean slate.

## Actions

Per row: mark reviewed, mark actioned, dismiss, or accept a suggestion — which creates a brief in [Content](/content/briefs). In bulk: set status, set severity, or export the selected rows to CSV.

Locked rows offer none of these.

## What "no gaps detected" means

It means this analysis found none in the answers it read. It is not a verification that every claim about you is true, and the page says so. Read it as "nothing stood out", not "we checked everything".
