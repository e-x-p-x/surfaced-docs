## What it means

The percentage of AI answers in the period where your brand was named in the text **or** linked to. It is the broadest of the five metrics: it asks whether you were present at all, not where, not how, and not whether the reader was told anything flattering.

## How it is calculated

```
brand visibility = responses mentioning your brand ÷ total responses collected
```

A response counts once whether it names you, links to you, or both. The two signals are merged deliberately — an answer that writes "Surfaced" and an answer that carries an inline link to `getsurfaced.ai` are both an appearance — and merged without double-counting, so the figure will never equal "mentions plus links".

The denominator is every **analysable** answer in the window: one that came back with text, or that the analysis could read. Empty and failed responses are in neither half of the fraction, so a bad run shrinks the sample rather than depressing the percentage.

One case is deliberately excluded from the numerator: an answer that cites a page on your domain without naming you in the text and without an inline link. That is a citation, not an appearance, and it is counted on [Sources](/insights/sources) instead.

## Worked example

From the [public demo](https://www.getsurfaced.ai/demo/insights/analytics?embed=1), which runs on a real workspace's data:

- Responses collected in the period: **2,140**
- Responses naming or linking the brand: **1,006**
- Brand visibility: 1,006 ÷ 2,140 = **47.0%**

The card's subtitle spells the fraction out — "1006 mentions in 2140 responses" — so you never have to take the percentage on trust.

## Where to find it

The first KPI card on [Analytics](/insights/analytics), with a trend chart below it and a per-platform breakdown beside that. The same figure appears per prompt on [Prompts](/insights/prompts), and per competitor on [Competitors](/insights/competitors).

## What it is not

**Not a share.** Two brands can both be named in the same answer, so visibility across every brand in your market sums to well over 100%. The metric that is a share is [share of voice](/metrics/share-of-voice).

**Not a ranking.** Being named last in a list of nine counts exactly the same as being the only recommendation. [Average position](/metrics/average-position) is the one that separates those.

**Not an endorsement.** Being named as the expensive option is an appearance. [Sentiment](/metrics/sentiment) is what tells you which kind.

**Not comparable across different prompt sets.** Visibility is measured over the prompts you chose. Adding ten prompts about a category you have no content for will lower it without anything having got worse.

<Warning>
  Brand visibility defaults to **unbranded** prompts, not all of them. On a workspace where that control is shown, the unfiltered figure is typically far lower than the blended one — because prompts that contain your own brand name almost always return it. [Periods and filters](/metrics/periods-and-filters) explains what you are looking at by default.
</Warning>
