Every metric on this page has its own page with four things: what it means in a sentence, the formula, a worked example with real arithmetic, and where in the app to find it.

They are all counted from the stored answers of a tracking run, so two metrics that disagree are usually counting over different sets of answers rather than contradicting each other.
