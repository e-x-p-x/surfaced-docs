Changes that affect what you see or how a number is calculated are recorded here. Scoring changes are called out explicitly, because a score that moves on a day you shipped nothing is otherwise impossible to explain.

<Update label="November 2026" description="Help centre">
  **This help centre.** Every screen, every metric with its formula and a worked example, and the behaviours that change what a number means — the anchored date window, the plan history floor, and the branded/unbranded default.

  Client documents moved from `docs.getsurfaced.ai` to `clients.getsurfaced.ai`. Existing links redirect; no access code changed.
</Update>

<Update label="October 2026" description="Insights">
  **Actions became one queue.** Work from gaps, the website audit and content performance now arrives in a single list with an owner and a state, instead of a to-do list on each screen. The sidebar badge counts the items assigned to you, not the workspace total.

  **Gap trends and Audit report** split what had been one screen into the two questions people actually ask: how findings are moving over time, and what to fix on the site right now.
</Update>

<Note>
  Entries before this page existed are not reconstructed here. For anything older, the release notes in-app remain the record.
</Note>
