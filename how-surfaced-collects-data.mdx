---
title: "How Surfaced collects data"
description: "What a tracking run actually does, and how often it happens."
---

Surfaced asks a fixed set of prompts to several AI assistants on a schedule, records each answer in full, and then reads the answers for brand mentions, competitor mentions and the sources the assistant cited.

The answers are the raw record. Every metric in the app is counted from them, which is why a metric can always be traced back to the answers it came from.
